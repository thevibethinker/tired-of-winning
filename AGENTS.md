The site is a single page (V, Nov 3 afternoon): countdown ticker, meter and seats, races grouped by poll-closing time with Contested/All, what-if; map and party room are dialog sheets; no receipts page (keep /api/receipts and logging). The state map separates live source calls from frozen forecasts. State colors summarize that chamber's races; partial calls are labeled with the uncalled count. The meter counts net flips across Senate, House and governors (V, Nov 3 ~10 PM): called GOP-held seats our listed D/I candidate won minus called D-held seats lost; six levels low energy (<5), winning (5–14), bigly (15–29), so much (30–44), tired (45–59), no more (60+). Senate, House and Governors cards sit at the same level of the hierarchy. Do not mark leads as wins to advance the meter. Governors (civicAPI 87535) have frozen Polymarket odds only, no model; definitions live in data/baseline-20261103/governor-addendum-20261104.json (rebuild via scripts/build_governor_addendum.py; log source corrections in it). Contested = 2–98% model or market odds, an uncalled count within 10 points, or an upset in progress. Once votes are in, races default to “What’s happening now” status groups (V, Nov 3 ~10:30 PM, blowout): flipped, long shots ahead, nail-biters, favorites behind, lost, too early (<25% in), as expected, called no flip, no votes. Called groups use source calls only; the “if every lead held” line never moves the meter. Name fixes go in data/baseline-20261103/name-corrections-20261104.json (dated addendum, baseline untouched); uncontested and D-vs-D seats are never losses. House flips and upsets are audible even in seats the model called safe. Governor calls use the same clips as the Senate.
